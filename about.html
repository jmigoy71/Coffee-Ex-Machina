WELCOME TO OUR CAFE

Discover Coffee Ex Machina

Coffee Ex Machina is a local coffee shop located in SJV7, Marinig, Cabuyao, Laguna. It offers a welcoming place where customers can enjoy flavorful beverages and delicious snacks without spending too much.

From iced coffee and frappes to refreshing coolers and satisfying snacks, the cafe provides a variety of choices for different cravings.

With its industrial-inspired atmosphere, warm lighting, and inviting environment, Coffee Ex Machina offers a place where friends and customers can relax and enjoy their coffee moments.

Our Mission

To provide delicious and affordable beverages and snacks while creating a welcoming environment where customers can enjoy quality coffee experiences.

Our Vision

To become a recognized local coffee destination known for its affordable products, enjoyable atmosphere, and memorable customer experiences.
